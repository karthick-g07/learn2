Streamline your team communication and collaboration with Zoho Cliq.  
With Zoho Cliq, you can:
- Chat with individuals or groups in real-time  
- Organize conversations with channels for teams, projects, or topics  
- Make audio and video calls directly from the app  
- Share files, images, and documents instantly  
- Integrate with Zoho apps and third-party tools for seamless workflows  
- Automate repetitive tasks using bots and custom commands  
- Access chats and data from desktop or mobile anytime  

Whether your team works in the office or remotely, Zoho Cliq keeps everyone connected, informed, and productive.
