Streamline expense reporting and gain complete control over your business spending with Zoho Expense.  
With Zoho Expense, you can:
- Capture receipts instantly using mobile or web upload  
- Automatically extract expense details with OCR technology  
- Set spending limits and enforce company expense policies  
- Track mileage with GPS-based distance calculation  
- Automate multi-level expense approvals  
- Generate detailed expense reports for better cost control  
- Integrate seamlessly with accounting, HR, and travel management tools  

From small purchases to large travel budgets, Zoho Expense helps you save time, prevent overspending, and ensure compliance with your financial policies.
